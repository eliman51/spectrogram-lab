Count individual voices

Groups calls of one kind by voice (pitch, pitch movement, spectral shape and YAMNet's sound embedding) and estimates how many distinct callers there are. Call type varies more than identity for many animals, so treat the count as an upper bound unless the calls are the same kind (all crows, all meows of one type).

Room sonar

Plays a 2-second rising sweep, listens to the echoes, and turns them into the room's impulse response. Echo delays give distances to surfaces, the decay gives reverb time and how furnished the room is, and a two-stage decay reveals an open doorway into another space.

  1. Stand near the middle of the room. Hold the phone at chest height in front of you, screen toward you, so your body blocks echoes from behind.
  2. Start a Voice Memos recording.
  3. Press Play test signal here, on this phone or on a second device held right against it.
  4. Face the first wall. After each pair of sweeps, turn a quarter turn to your right when the screen says so. The beeps count the heading (1 to 4).
  5. Stop the recording and open it with Analyze recording.
