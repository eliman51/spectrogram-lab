Sonar Recorder Spectrogram Lab

Records the room while it plays the sweep, with the phone's echo cancellation and noise reduction turned off, then maps the room from the echoes. Companion to Spectrogram Lab.

  1. Stand near the middle of the room. Hold the phone at chest height in front of you, screen toward you.
  2. Turn the volume all the way up and face the first wall.
  3. Tap Start sonar run and allow the microphone.
  4. When the screen says so, turn a quarter turn to your right. The beeps count the heading (1 to 4).
